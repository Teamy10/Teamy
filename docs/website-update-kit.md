## 7. FAQ updates

Add these new questions to the existing FAQ, after the current questions. Do not change any other existing question except the two listed under "Update these".

### New questions

**What does the WhatsApp AI agent actually do?**
It confirms COD orders, answers customer questions about orders, shipping, sizing and returns, reminds shoppers about abandoned carts, and sends reorder and restock alerts, all on your own WhatsApp Business number.

**Do I need to rebuild my store to add the AI agent?**
No. The AI Agent Add-on connects to your existing store. Nothing about your current website changes.

**Will the AI say something wrong to my customers?**
The agent answers only from your store data and the policies you approve. When it isn't sure, it hands the chat to your team. You can see every conversation.

**What does WhatsApp charge?**
Meta charges small fees for some business messages on the WhatsApp Business Platform. These are billed to your account at cost. Teamy doesn't add a markup.

**How do payments work?**
For stores, 50% to start and 50% before launch. For the AI Agent Add-on, the setup fee and first month are paid upfront. We accept UPI, cards and net banking via Razorpay, and international clients can pay via PayPal.
